# UDA
Pseudo-labelling is sensitive to the value of the threshold. A low threshold results in many false positive pseduo-labels, while a high threshold yields many false negative pseudo-labels.

Idea: threshold in two directions and only provide supervision on the confident regions.

![alt text](../images/weights_epoch20.png)
![alt text](../images/weights_epoch3.jpg)

Unfortunately, even with weighted mse, the student seems to overfit to the teacher "confident" regions. In the two images below, taken from different epochs, we can see that the uncertain regions shrink as training progresses.

![](../images/multiviewx_overfit1_uda.jpg)
![](../images/multiviewx_overfit_uda.jpg)


Other alternatives:
- soft labels?
- mix of pseudo-labels and soft labels. E.g., pseudo-labels in confident regions, soft labels in uncertain regions
